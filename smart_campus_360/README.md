# Smart Campus 360 - AI-Powered Smart University Management Platform
> **Tagline:** *"One Campus. One Intelligent Platform."*

Smart Campus 360 is an enterprise-grade university management platform with dual dedicated portals:
1. **Student Portal (`student.html`):** Tailored from the student's point of view for continuous academic tracking, early alerts, personalized learning recovery plans, QR exam hall tickets, digital growth passport portfolios, campus issue reporting with verified impact scores, innovation exchange challenges, and confidential wellbeing support.
2. **Administrator Portal (`admin.html` / `index.html`):** The comprehensive institutional command center for admissions officers, examination coordinators, payroll & HR managers, and campus operations.

## 🎓 Page 1: Student Success & Innovation Portal (`student.html`)
Designed entirely from the **Student Point of View**:
- **Welcome & Identity:** Displays student profile (`Aarav Sharma`, ID: `SC360-2026-CS001`, B.Tech CSE, 3rd Sem, Section A).
- **Persona Switcher:** Switch between *Aarav Sharma* (High Performer), *Rohan Sharma* (Attendance Alert <75%), and *Meera Nair* (Published Researcher) to test different student states.
- **Attendance & Early Academic Warning:** Live gauge with 75% cutoff compliance check. If attendance drops below 75%, an Early Academic Support warning banner activates.
- **Personalized Learning Recovery Plan (Section 5.4):** Interactive mentor-curated milestones (Peer study circle, assignment revision, faculty office hours) with interactive check-boxes.
- **Academics & Exams:** View enrolled subject credits, current grades, and examination schedule.
- **Printable Exam Hall Ticket (Section 3.5):** Generates an official examination pass with candidate details, desk assignment, and QR code verification.
- **Digital Student Growth Passport (Section 5.6):** Badges for verified hackathons, certifications, research papers, and technical skills, with a **One-Click Export Career Portfolio (PDF)** button for job applications.
- **Campus Opportunity Matcher™ (Section 5.7):** AI-matched internships, fellowships, and hackathons tailored to the student's skill tags.
- **Campus Issue Reporting & Verified Impact Score™ (Section 4 & 5.9):** Students submit maintenance/IT/accessibility tickets. When technicians complete a repair, students physically verify the fix on site (**"Confirm Fix on Site"**) to close the ticket.
- **Student-Powered Innovation Exchange (Section 3):** View live student engineering pilots (e.g., Team EcoVolt's verified 35.1% energy reduction) and join open challenges.
- **Smart Resource-Sharing Network (Section 7):** Request equipment borrowing for 3D printers, digital oscilloscopes, and survey drones.
- **Campus Pulse (Section 8):** Anonymous 1-click daily feedback for food, Wi-Fi, A/C, and library spaces.
- **Confidential Wellbeing Check-In (Section 5.8):** Private support requests for academic stress, peer guidance, or career counseling.

- **Automated Admissions & Enrollment (Module 1):** Applicant management, simulated OCR marksheet extraction, deterministic eligibility rule checking, official offer letters, and automatic student ID generation.

- **Employee Operations & Smart Substitute Finder (Module 5):** Staff roster, biometric check-in feeds, leave management, and the Smart Substitute Finder matching absent faculty with 98% domain fit.
- **Campus Problem Radar & What-If Simulator (Module 6):** Flags recurring asset breakdowns and features an interactive slider calculator for compaction %, HVAC shift, and solar pavement with real-time ₹ savings and CO2 offset.
- **Immutable Campus Audit Trail (Module 7):** NIST AI RMF trustworthiness metrics and cryptographic sequencing of every administrative action.
- **Run Viva Demo:** Automated 10-step guided presentation script.
